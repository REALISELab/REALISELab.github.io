
## Leonardo Sergio Centellas Claros

I am a research visitor at the REALISE Lab, Concordia University, under the supervision of Professor [Diego Elias Costa](/teamInfo/diego). I am affiliated with the Department of Computer Science, School of Engineering, at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile in Santiago, Chile.

### Research Interest

My research interests lie in software engineering, with a focus on code review automation and the quality of training data for machine learning models in software engineering tasks. I also research how developers adopt new technologies, particularly AI coding agents and the mechanisms used to configure them.
